DFRobot EU868 Gateway

The network backbone of the Ayak system. The DFRobot EU868 LoRaWAN Gateway bridges hundreds of field-deployed sensor nodes directly to the cloud over a 15 km line-of-sight radius — while consuming minimal power and supporting solar-panel operation for fully off-grid deployments.

FIELD SENSOR

Gravity: IP65 Soil Moisture Sensor

The eyes of the system. This capacitive soil moisture sensor is deployed directly in the earth, delivering corrosion-free, precise volumetric water content readings over its gold-plated ENIG probe. The IP65 waterproof rating guarantees reliable readings through seasons of rain, irrigation, and harsh desert conditions.
